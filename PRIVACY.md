# Privacy Policy — CopyClean

_Last updated: 2026-06-09_

CopyClean does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any data. It runs
entirely on your own machine.

## What it accesses

- **Your local Claude Code session transcript files** (under `~/.claude/projects/`):
  read only, and only to find the most recent block of text or code to copy.
- **Your operating system clipboard:** CopyClean places the text you asked to copy
  onto your local clipboard (via `pbcopy`, `xclip`, `wl-copy`, `xsel`, or
  `clip.exe`).

## What it does NOT do

- No network requests of any kind.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or usage reporting.
- No third-party services or dependencies.
- No data leaves your machine. The only place content goes is your own clipboard,
  where you chose to put it.

## Data retention

CopyClean stores nothing. It reads a transcript file, copies the selected text to
your clipboard, and exits.
